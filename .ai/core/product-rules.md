# Product Rules

- Freediving is safety-sensitive. Avoid UX or copy that implies guaranteed safety, certification, medical advice, or official endorsement unless the product explicitly provides it.
- Treat instructor, school, site, buddy, and trip data as trust-bearing information. Verification and provenance matter.
- Do not invent business rules to fill gaps. If a phase needs a product decision, hard-stop.
- Avoid dark patterns in booking, applications, reviews, or trust flows.
- Favor Philippine geography, local diving terminology, and community norms over generic travel-site assumptions.
- Public-facing flows should reduce spam and low-quality submissions without making legitimate community contribution painful.

Product decisions should be recorded in `.ai/state/decisions.md` only when they are durable enough to constrain future implementation.
