`freediving.ph` is a community platform for freedivers in the Philippines. It combines social identity, coordination, discovery, and freediving-specific trust surfaces.

Current intended product surfaces:

- social profiles with explicit visibility controls
- private 1:1 messaging with request gating
- buddy relationships and buddy finder
- groups and thread-style community discussion
- Chika/forum discussions with pseudonymous-per-thread behavior
- dive-site exploration and moderated site submissions
- events with visibility-aware discovery and RSVP
- competitive records with verification states
- homepage mixed feed backed by backend-owned ranking
- public SEO/content surfaces for guides, features, locations, and about pages

Future-but-not-core launch surfaces named in the legacy spec:

- training logs
- safety resources
- ocean awareness content
- marketplace
- collaboration hub
